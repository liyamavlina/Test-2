Educational Programme: Texts, languages, and digital tools

The programme combines the traditions of philology and linguistics with modern technologies, offering students a unique opportunity to master skills in demand in the digital age. Programme graduates gain in-depth knowledge of textual studies, language and their role in culture, as well as practical skills to create and promote language products in the digital environment. Students can build their own educational trajectory, choosing from a wide range of disciplines, fields of study, and projects. The training process focuses on developing professional competencies, such as proficiency in several foreign languages, working with language data, and applying innovative approaches in the educational and communication fields.
